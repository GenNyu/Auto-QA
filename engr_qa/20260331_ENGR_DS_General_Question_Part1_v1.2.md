### Question:
For each role, how are security responsibilities documented and maintained? (e.g., Security Officer (SO), Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), security operations, risk management, etc.)
### Answer:
1. Responsibilities and authorities of the general director: 
+ Authorize the deputy general director to be in charge of technology, 
+ perform the responsibilities and authority of the general director in the company's information security management system.
2. Responsibilities of deputy general manager, who is in charge of technology, concurrently head of information security: 
+ Ensure that responsibilities and authorities for the relevant position are assigned and communicated throughout the organization. 
+ The person with the highest responsibility is the person in charge of all information security activities of the company. 
+ Appoint a representative to manage the information security system. 
+ Develop and strengthen information security and management regime, approve company policies, approve spending budgets and the handbook for management process. 
+ Chair the meeting to review, supervise and inspect of potential information security threats. + Provide human and financial resources to fulfill the requirements of the management system.
3. Responsibilities and authorities of ISMS system manager, concurrently deputy head of information security department: 
+ Based on ISO 27001:2013 standard, he/she will establish, implement and maintain information security system requirements. 
+ In charge of regulating and communicating the operation of internal and external systems. 
+ Consolidate reports on the effectiveness of the information security management system with BOD, in order to review and improve the provided data on the information security management system. 
+ Implement the company's management policy and monitor the achievement of objectives and spendings. Regulating the exchange of information security related information with internal and external parties. Ensuring the awareness of information security management in the organization and meet the requirements of all stakeholders. Monitoring and supervising risk analysis & assessment, and control measures for risks and opportunities.
4. Responsibilities and authorities of the deputy head of the Information Security Department Managing information security incidents. Managing the company's business continuity
5. Responsibility and authorities of information security committee 
+ Responsible for activities to promote information security management in different departments. 
+ Assess the impact of information security to our company and manage risk and emergency incidents. 
+ Set goals and budgets for information security. 
+ Implement internal assessment; inspect, monitor, and report to the management representative. 
+ Grasp the management status of information security factors, unexpected incidents of departments, if they are not in accordance with the management plan on information security spending policies and objectives, it is necessary to take appropriate action. 
+ Establish the company's emergency response team, develop and implement the company's emergency response plan, when an emergency occurs, it should be reported to the IT manager. 
+ The head of IT department is responsible for reporting to the company's management in case of necessity. He/she will be in charge of communicating information security policies and objectives to employees, exchanging information with external parties, supporting departments to organize training related to information security awareness.
### Comment:
6. Responsibility and authority of department managers 
+ Responsible for managing and promoting information security, do not disclose and leak confidential information, ensuring no loss of company assets. 
+ Based on the company's information security management policies, goals and plans, develop a detailed target management plan for the department and implement it together with relevant departments. 
+ Understand thoroughly the activities of the department. If it is not in line with the policies and plans, it is necessary to find out the causes and implement countermeasures.
+ Summarize the management activities of the department, report to the head of the Information Security Department in periodic meetings and the general director of the company. 
+ When the department has an emergency or unexpected incident, it is necessary to grasp the situation, make a report, and coordinate with relevant departments to respond and protect evidence related to the incident. Submitting the investigation report to the information security department, confirming the exact cause and preventive measures, implement and confirm the effectiveness. 
+ Coordinate department employees to compile work instructions related to information security management and conduct training for all department employees. + Responsible for identifying, analyzing, evaluating and implementing rish control measures.
