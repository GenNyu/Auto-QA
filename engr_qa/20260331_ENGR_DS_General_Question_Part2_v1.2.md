### Question:
How does your organization classify risk? (e.g., What criteria (e.g. likelihood, impact) form the foundation of risk assessments in your organization?)
### Answer:
We classify risk based on risk probability (rare, unlikely, occasionally, highly likely, certain) and risk impact (insignificant, Medium, High, Critical & Extreme)
After identified the probability and the impact of threats/ vulnerabilities, ISMR will identify the level of risk based on the formula:
Level of risk = probability x impact
