### Question:
Has your organization, or any of your third parties providing services to you, suffered a data breach in the last 12 months? If yes, please provide details. 
Do you have a process or solution in place for Data Loss Prevention (DLP) to protect AIA sensitive data from data exfiltration?
If yes, does your DLP process or solution enforce the following technology controls?
- Data discovery and tagging as per classification of data
- Restricted use of removable devices by disabling media ports by default or use of Endpoint DLP
- Email domain whitelisting or use of Email DLP
- Website blacklisting (of public messaging and file storage/sharing sites) or use of Web DLP
- Network scanning of files and data stream
- CASB for cloud hosted assets
- Shadow IT discovery and remediation
- Real time monitoring and response
How do you equip your network to identify data breaches and respond to them?

### Evidence:
UrBox's data loss prevention policy is mentioned in IT Security Policy & Information risk management procedure, for example:
MOBILE AND REMOTE WORKING DEVICES
The use of mobile devices (laptops, tablets, and smartphones) to access company data systems and remote work needs to be managed with device management, to protect data assets and prevent the disclosure exit or modify, delete data.
Use should be restricted to business purposes and users should be aware of and accept terms and conditions of use. Especially responsible for securing data in the equipment assigned.
Employees who use mobile devices to access corporate data systems must be aware of the IT security issues involved and implement appropriate safeguards to minimize the risk. Employees are responsible for the safe use and preservation, prevention of damage, loss and theft of mobile equipment. All mobile devices accessing the company's data system must be allowed by the IT to ensure compatibility and security of data with all data systems being used at the company.
Mobile devices can only be used outside of the workplace, at home or in other locations, with the permission of the CEO or authorized person. This use is only for the business purposes of the company, and the user must know and accept the terms and conditions of use, including acceptable and appropriate privacy practices.
Users are responsible if their mobile device is spreading, attacking internal and external network services.
Users are not allowed to store personal data; data is prohibited under the regulation on mobile devices.
All mobile devices provided by the company and data stored in these devices are owned by the company and may be recovered without notice to the user.
PROVISIONS ON THE EMPLOYEE'S LIABILITY
Access requests must be approved in writing and users must sign the commitment to IT procedures and policies or NDA upon request to provide system resources (Email, Internet, Fax, FTP, USB, etc.). )
Users must not use access data (ID, password) of others unless authorized by the competent authorities in accordance with the company. Not to be illegally logged into the company's data system or intentionally pass or violate the applicable inspection procedures.
MANAGEMENT OF ENCRYPTION
Adopts the encryption measures required to protect the confidentiality, authenticity or integrity of data.
Information about passwords and other sensitive data should be encrypted when transmitted over the network and stored.
Encryption solutions used must be regularly checked, re-evaluated the level of safety and timely handling of weaknesses if any.
Encryption keys need to be backed up and stored in case of need to recover when the encryption key is lost.
Prevention of malicious code
IT support service department must deploy and maintain anti-virus system, malicious code and promptly update the samples of new viruses and malicious code for the whole data system.
Users do not open email messages, attachments or links in unsolicited email origin to avoid infection of the malicious code.
Do not visit websites that are of unclear, suspicious origin (unhealthy websites, games, movies, etc.).
Users must immediately report to the support service in case of detection but not destroy the virus, malicious code.
Users are not willing to download, install the free software without the permission of the system administrator.
