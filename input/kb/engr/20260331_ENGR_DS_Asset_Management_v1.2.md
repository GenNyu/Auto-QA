### Question:
Describe how your organization securely handles, transfers, stores, and disposes both removable media (e.g., Hard drives, CD/DVD, memory sticks, etc.) and printed material.
### Answer:
1. Removable media storage devices (removable, unfixed devices inside a computer such as magnetic tapes, optical discs, external hard drives, or USB flash drives, memory cards, etc) are not allowed to use. Exceptions need to get the approval of the management team
2. Users and data managers of these devices must take full responsibility for the data stored on its.
3. Storage media that are no longer in use must be destroyed to ensure that they cannot be reused and cannot be restored into internal memory.
4. The process of destroying these devices must be documented and kept on file for retrieval when necessary

Yes

### Question:
How is media in transport protected against unauthorised access, misuse or corruption, and damage?
Key considerations:
i. Is transport facilitated by an authorized courier?
ii. Is tracking of assets in-transit performed to ensure successful transfers?
iii. Are assets encrypted at the hardware level when in-transit?
iv. Are assets in-transit sufficiently protected against overheating and excess moisture?
### Answer:
1. Media must be properly packed to avoid possible physical damage during transportation and in accordance with the manufacturer's technical requirements for preservation and transportation.
2. The transportation of those devices must be fully recorded, the sending/receiving time of each stage in the transportation process.
3. If an outside transportation service is needed, UrBox will hire a reliable transportation or delivery service
