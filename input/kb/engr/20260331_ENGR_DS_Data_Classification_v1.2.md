### Question:
Describe how your organization classifies information (i.e. based on its value, legal requirements, sensitivity, or criticality to the organization) and gives employees direction on how information in each classification is to be handled.
### Answer:
1. Public information: Including information such as company name and address, description of product and service that a company provide. These information can be shared publicly and do not have to follow any guideline.
2. Internal information: Including information such as email, employee account information, internal working process and work instructions. Employees should only share this information with colleages who have demand and the access right to the information. Non-sensitive information but the source of information can only be found at the company, will be disseminated within the company.
3. Sensitive/Confidential Information: Includes information such as customer personal information and financial information. This information should be protected from unauthorized access and should only be shared in case there is a demand and legitimate access right.
