## D. Structured Output
### Question:
Does the organization have a dedicated information security position? (Officer/Manager)
### Answer:
UrBox has a dedicated information security team responsible for ensuring the security of information and data.

---
### Question:
Does the organization have a formal written Information Security Policies?
### Answer:
UrBox has established formal written Information Security Policies that outline the guidelines and procedures for maintaining the security of information and data within the organization.

---
### Question:
Does your organization conduct risk assessments at least annually?
### Answer:
our company conducts risk assessments at least annually to identify potential security vulnerabilities and assess the overall risk landscape. This helps us proactively address any potential threats and ensure the ongoing security of our systems and data.

---
### Question:
Does the organization take any third-party external Information Security assessment in the past 2 years?
### Answer:
within the past 2 years, UrBox has undergone third-party external Information Security assessments to ensure an objective evaluation of our security measures and practices. This helps us identify any areas that might require improvement and enhances the overall security posture of our organization.

---
### Question:
Does your organization have policy for third-party management?
### Answer:
our company has a third-party management policy in place. This policy outlines the procedures and guidelines for evaluating, selecting, and managing third-party vendors and partners. It includes criteria for assessing the security and privacy practices of these third parties to ensure that they meet our organization's standards and requirements. Regular monitoring and review of third-party relationships are also part of this policy to maintain a strong level of security and minimize potential risks associated with external partners.

---
### Question:
Are background checks conducted for all perspective employees who have access to sensitive information?
### Answer:
Urbox conducts background checks for all prospective employees who will have access to sensitive information. This practice is part of our security protocols to ensure that individuals with appropriate credentials and trustworthiness are granted access to sensitive data and resources. Background checks help mitigate potential risks and safeguard the confidentiality of the information handled by our employees.

---
### Question:
Are employees required to attend mandatory annual information security training?
### Answer:
employees in our company are required to attend mandatory annual information security training. This training is designed to keep employees updated on the latest security threats, best practices, and protocols for maintaining the security of our systems, data, and information. By regularly educating our staff, we enhance their awareness and understanding of security measures, contributing to a more secure work environment.

---
### Question:
Does your organization implement network firewall protection?
### Answer:
we use routers and ACLs for local area including workstations and use Cloud Server (with firewall)

---
### Question:
Does your organization implement web application firewall protection?
### Answer:
our company implements web application firewall (WAF) protection in AWS Cloudflare. A WAF is designed to monitor, filter, and block malicious traffic directed at web applications. It helps protect against various attacks targeting vulnerabilities in web applications, such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and more.

---
### Question:
Have you implemented monitoring and alerting for your network?
### Answer:
our company has implemented Granfana systems to monitor and alert our network. These systems continuously monitor network traffic, system activities, and other relevant metrics to detect any unusual or suspicious behavior. If any anomalies or potential security breaches are identified, the monitoring system triggers alerts to our security team, allowing for immediate investigation and response. This proactive approach helps us maintain the security and integrity of our network infrastructure.

---
### Question:
Do you operate a VPN that allows remote access to your network?
### Answer:
our comapny operates a Virtual Private Network (VPN) that allows remote access to our network. A VPN creates a secure and encrypted connection between remote devices and our internal network, enabling authorized users to access resources as if they were physically present within our organization's premises.
